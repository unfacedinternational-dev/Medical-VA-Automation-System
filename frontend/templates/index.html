How the Medical VA Automation System Works

The system connects the routine administrative steps of a medical VA workflow into one organized operational process.

1
PatientsCreate and maintain patient records so appointments, tasks, billing, insurance, documents and follow-ups stay connected.
2
AppointmentsSchedule visits, track status and identify what needs to happen before and after each appointment.
3
Appointment completed → automated workflowCompletion moves the process forward: billing record → insurance check → task creation → staff notification → claim/payment tracking.
4
Tasks & follow-upsRequired actions become visible work items with priorities and due dates, reducing missed follow-ups and manual tracking.
5
Billing & insuranceTrack eligibility, claims, payment status and follow-up work from the same patient-centered workflow.
6
Documents & filesUpload and organize patient-related files so the operational record and its supporting documents stay together.

How it helps the VA

  • Less repetitive manual tracking
  • Clear priorities and work queues
  • Fewer missed follow-ups
  • Centralized operational records
  • Clear visibility of completed work

How it helps the employer

  • Visibility into workflow status
  • Consistent administrative processes
  • Easier monitoring of claims and tasks
  • Centralized operational information
  • Better reporting and accountability
Automation logic: Patient → Appointment → Completion → Billing/Insurance → Tasks & Notifications → Claim/Payment Tracking → Reporting.
